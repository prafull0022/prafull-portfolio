About Me

Precision engineering, human-centered delivery.

Civil Engineer with a Bachelors in Civil Engineering and a Masters in Structural Engineering, combining strong academic foundations with hands-on industry experience across highway engineering and independent civil engineering practice.

I began my career as a Senior Highway Engineer, where I worked on the planning, design, and execution of road and highway projects. My responsibilities included geometric design, pavement design, drainage systems, quantity estimation, site supervision, and coordination with contractors and stakeholders to ensure projects were delivered on time, within budget, and to specification.

After gaining solid field and design experience, I transitioned into self-employment as a Civil Engineer, independently managing civil and structural projects. This role sharpened my skills in client handling, project planning, cost estimation, structural design, and end-to-end project execution — from concept to completion.

I'm passionate about delivering quality engineering work and am open to opportunities in structural design, highway engineering, and civil project management.
